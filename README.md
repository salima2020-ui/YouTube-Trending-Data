Technologies Used
Python 3.x: Primary language used for data manipulation and analysis.
Pandas: Used for data loading, deduplication, missing value handling, feature engineering, and statistical aggregation.
NumPy: Used for numerical operations and array transformations.
Matplotlib: Native data visualization library used to create distribution plots, scatter diagrams, category bar charts, line plots, correlation heatmaps, and box plots (built strictly without Seaborn).
Key Insights & Findings
1. Text Optimization & Title Length
Optimal Title Length: The majority of trending videos feature titles between 40 to 70 characters. Extremely short or excessively long titles appear significantly less frequently among top-performing content.
Video Descriptions: High-performing videos maintain detailed descriptions, providing search algorithms with key contextual terms and audience links.
2. Category Performance & Tag Strategy
Dominant Categories: Entertainment, Music, and People & Blogs consistently account for the largest share of trending videos.
Tag Usage: While tag count alone does not guarantee higher view counts, relevant tagging helps indexing algorithms deliver content to the appropriate target audience.
3. Publishing Strategy (Time & Day)
Publishing Hour: Videos published during peak audience activity hours experience faster initial engagement and reach trending status sooner.
Day of Week: Viewership patterns fluctuate predictably between weekdays and weekends, reflecting changes in routine user engagement.
4. Audience Engagement & Disabled Features
Interactivity Correlation: High view counts strongly correlate with elevated likes and comments. A high like-to-view ratio reflects strong audience satisfaction and viewer retention.
Disabled Comments/Ratings: Disabling interaction features does not directly penalize total views, but it limits overall user engagement and community activity.
5. Trend Latency (Days to Trend)
Time to Reach Trending: The vast majority of trending videos achieve trending status within 1 to 3 days after publication. Late-blooming trends occurring after extended periods are statistically rare.
